Ranks, Stars, and Gold

Three ranks

  • Raider

    The friendly rank. Rivals are slow to react and send small attacks. The most generous par time.

  • Jarl

    The middle rank. You will need a real plan.

  • Berserker

    The toughest rank. Rivals attack early, team up their buildings, pounce on anything you leave empty, and keep their eyes on you. The fastest par time.

Rivals never cheat. They see what you see and play by the same rules.

Par times and stars

Every rank on every level has a par time. Win faster than par to earn that rank’s star. That makes 3 stars per level, and 216 in the whole campaign.

A harder rank counts for the easier ones. Win on Berserker and the win also counts on Jarl and Raider. Beat the Berserker par and you earn all three stars at once.

Stars you earn are yours to keep. They open new worlds: see The Worlds.

Gold

Gold is what you spend in the store. You earn it by winning.

  • Your first win on each rank of a level pays 25 gold on Raider, 45 on Jarl, and 80 on Berserker. A Berserker win counts on the easier ranks too, so a first Berserker win can pay all three at once.
  • Beat your best time by a real margin (at least 2 seconds, or 5% for longer levels) to earn a little more. Each level and rank has a limit on this bonus.
  • Ordinary replays pay no gold. The results screen always tells you why.
  • Optional ads pay 500 gold each. Watching is always your choice.

The store

Open the store from the world map. Spend gold on:

  • Upgrades. Eight upgrades, five steps each, that make your side a little stronger: Defence, Speed, Strength, Rate of Fire, Procreation, Birth Rate, Spell Power, and Blast Size. They help in single-player battles, never in multiplayer.
  • Spell refills. 3 more charges of any spell.
  • Opening the way early. Stuck? You can open the next level of a world with gold (from Greenwood 5 on), or open the next world before you have its stars.

Everything in the store can be earned by playing.
