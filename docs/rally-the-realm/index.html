Tap. Send. Conquer.

Every level is a hand-painted map dotted with buildings and joined by winding roads. Your buildings are red. Each one grows little Vikings. Tap one of yours, tap a target, and half your troops march out to take it.

Three rival clans, blue, green, and yellow, want the same land. Capture their buildings, defend your own, and be the last clan standing. Battles take a few minutes, so there is always time for one more.

5 kinds of buildings

Houses, castles, forges, rune towers, and stables, each with its own trick.

4 spells

Fireball, Rally, Shield, and Freeze can turn a battle in a heartbeat.

3 ranks

Raider, Jarl, and Berserker. Beat each rank’s par time for a star.

72 levels

Eight painted worlds, from quiet forests to roaring volcanoes.

33 trophies

Fill your Trophy Hall with feats, world prizes, and milestones.

Play with friends

Invite up to three friends or family through Game Center and battle together.
