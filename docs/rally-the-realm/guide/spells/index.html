Spells

  • Fireball

    Cast it on a building that is not yours to blast out half of its troops. Cast it on open ground to hit every soldier near the blast, and careful: that includes yours.

  • Rally

    All your marching troops move half again as fast for 8 seconds. Great for winning a race to an empty building.

  • Shield

    One of your buildings cannot be hurt or captured for 8 seconds. Save it for the moment a big attack lands.

  • Freeze

    Every enemy soldier on the road stops in place for 8 seconds. Perfect for buying time to fill a building.

Charges

  • Each spell has its own number of charges. The number on its button shows how many you have left. Each cast uses one.
  • Every new saga starts with 3 free charges of each spell.
  • After you cast a spell, it rests for 10 seconds before you can cast it again.
  • Charges you do not use carry over to your next battle.
  • Out of charges? Buy a refill of 3 in the store with gold you win in battle.
  • Rivals never cast spells.

Win any level without casting a spell to earn the Bare Fists trophy.
