About Funston Games

A small, independent game studio.

Funston Games makes games for iPhone and iPad. We like games that are easy to pick up, quick to play, and full of little decisions that matter.

Our first game is Rally the Realm, a real-time battle game with chunky cartoon Vikings and hand-painted maps. Every level was playtested again and again until it felt just right.

What we believe

  • Fair fun. Everything in our games can be earned by playing. Buying something is a shortcut, never the only way.
  • Your information stays yours. We never sell anything about you, and if a game shows ads, whether they are personalized is your choice.
  • Polish matters. We would rather ship one game that feels great than five that feel rushed.
