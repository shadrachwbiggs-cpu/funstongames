Raid Valhalla

Nine raids across the realms of Norse legend. The hardest battles in the game.

How to open it

Raid Valhalla opens when you have earned every star in the campaign: all three stars on every level of all eight worlds. Until then, its tile on the world map shows how many stars you have so far. Once a raid is beaten, the next one opens.

The nine realms

  1. Asgard
  2. Vanaheim
  3. Alfheim
  4. Midgard
  5. Jotunheim
  6. Svartalfheim
  7. Niflheim
  8. Helheim
  9. Muspelheim, the hardest level in the whole game

Raid rules

  • Raids are played on Berserker only, so each raid has one star to win.
  • Every raid is tougher than any campaign level. Asgard is brutal on purpose.
  • Raids pay gold like any other level, and raid stars are yours to keep, but they do not open worlds.
  • Beat 9 different raids to earn the Valkyrie Spear trophy.
