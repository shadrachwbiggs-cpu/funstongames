Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Rally the Realm is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Funston Games, LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game keeps, what it shares, and what it never collects. It applies to the Rally the Realm app. Our website has its own website privacy notice.

The short version

  • We have no accounts and no servers that store your data, and we do not sell anything about you. The game keeps your progress on your device, not with us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob unless you have the Jarl’s Share subscription. Google collects some information to show them, as explained below. Whether those ads are personalized is up to you, and children never get personalized ads.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see either copy.
  • Multiplayer and sign-in use Apple’s Game Center. Purchases, when they arrive, use Apple’s App Store. Apple handles those under its own privacy policy, and we never see your Apple ID, password, or payment details.

What the game saves

  • Your sagas: the saga names you type, and each saga’s stars, gold, upgrades, spell charges, trophies, best times, and unlocked levels.
  • Your settings, such as music and sound volume.
  • Levels you build in the level editor, and any picture you choose to use as a map.

All of this is saved on your device. We cannot see it. If your device backs up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include the game’s saves, under Apple’s terms.

iCloud saves

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your sagas in your own iCloud account, so your progress follows you between your iPhone and iPad and comes back if you delete and reinstall the game. Apple stores this copy under its own terms and privacy policy. It is private to your Apple account, and we cannot see it. Your settings and the levels and pictures in your level editor are not part of it. If you do not use iCloud, your progress stays only on your device.

Photos

The level editor lets you pick a picture from your photo library to use as a map. The game uses Apple’s photo picker, so it only ever receives the one picture you choose, never the rest of your library. A copy of that picture is kept inside the game on your device.

Sharing levels

If you export a level you built, the game makes a level file and opens Apple’s share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example AirDrop, Messages, or Files. We do not receive it.

Game Center and multiplayer

The game signs you in to Apple’s Game Center so friends can invite you to battles. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple’s privacy policy covers it.

When you play an online battle, the devices in that battle send each other, through Game Center:

  • your saga name, which the other players see during the battle and on the results card,
  • the version number of the game, so everyone plays the same version,
  • the moves each player makes in the battle, and small checks that keep every device in step.

The game uses the player identifier Game Center provides to tell players apart during a battle. Invites are sent by Apple to Game Center friends or as a link in Messages that you send yourself. None of this information is sent to us or stored by us, and nothing is kept after the battle ends. Pick a saga name you are happy for friends to see.

Ads

Between some levels, the game shows an ad. You can also choose to watch an ad for gold. Ads never appear during a battle, and the Jarl’s Share subscription removes the ads between levels. Ads come from Google AdMob, run by Google, and Google’s policies explain how it uses information from apps like ours.

To show ads and measure them, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking (see below),
  • your IP address and rough location worked out from it, and details such as your device type, its language, and the app you are using,
  • which ads you see and tap, and how the ad service performs, including crash information.

We receive reports about how ads perform overall. They do not tell us who you are.

Your choices about ads. iPhone and iPad ask whether the game may track you. If you say yes, ads may be personalized using activity from other companies’ apps and websites. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this any time in your device’s Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you also get a privacy message from Google to make your choices.

Your age. Before the first ad, the game asks what year you were born. The answer stays on your device and is not sent to us or to Google. It is used only to decide how ads work for you: for players under 13, the game never asks to track, and tells Google to treat ad requests as coming from a child, so ads are not personalized.

Purchases and subscriptions

The game offers gold packs and the Jarl’s Share subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles every payment. We never see your card or bank details. Apple tells the game what you bought so it can deliver it, and Apple gives us sales reports that do not identify you.

Checks with Apple

The game asks Apple whether it was installed from the App Store or from TestFlight, Apple’s test service. Apple answers the app directly and does not share anything about you with us.

If you turned on sharing with app developers in your device’s privacy settings, Apple may send us crash reports and performance data that do not identify you. If you test the game through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple’s TestFlight terms.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. For players who tell the game they are under 13, ads are not personalized and the game never asks to track them, as explained under Ads. Online battles are by invite only, through Game Center, where parents can use Apple’s Screen Time settings to limit multiplayer and friends. If you think a child has given us personal information, please email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete a saga, or restart it, from inside the game. This also applies to the copy in your iCloud.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it saved on that device. Your iCloud copy stays and returns if you reinstall, so delete your sagas in the game first if you want them gone everywhere.
  • Sign out of Game Center, or limit multiplayer, in your device’s Settings.
  • Say no to tracking, or change your answer in your device’s Settings. Or get the Jarl’s Share, which removes the ads between levels.
  • We keep no personal information about you ourselves, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. Google’s own controls cover what it holds. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and we will delete it on request.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds something that changes this policy, such as saving progress on our own servers or downloading new levels from our own servers, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
