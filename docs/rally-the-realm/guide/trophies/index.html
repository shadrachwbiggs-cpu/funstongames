Trophies

33 trophies wait in the Trophy Hall, reached from the world map. Each saga earns its own.

Feats

Lucky RuneWin your first level.
Bare FistsWin a level without casting a spell.
Raven FeatherBeat a Berserker par time.
Rune StoneUpgrade a rune tower to its highest tier.
Golden GobletEarn all 3 stars on every level of one world.
Wolf PeltWin on Berserker without losing a single building.
Swift OarWin a level in under 60 seconds.
Shield WallHold 3 castles at 50 at the same moment.
Mended ShieldOwn 5 buildings, fall to just 1, then win the level.

Milestones

Bear ClawCapture 100 buildings in total.
Fire StaffCast 25 fireballs.
Mead HornWin 10 levels in a row without a loss.
War HornCapture 1,000 buildings in total.
Smith's HammerMax out every upgrade in the store.
Mapmaker's QuillSave your first custom level in the level editor.
All-Father's EyeEarn every star in all 8 worlds.
Iron OathFinish the campaign in a No Spells saga.
Grandfather's AxeFinish the campaign in a No Upgrades saga.
The Long RoadFinish the campaign in a No Gold Unlocks saga.
Mask of FuryFinish the campaign in a Berserker Only saga.
Wanderer's HoodFinish the campaign in a Fog of War saga.
Gleipnir UnboundFinish a Ragnarok Saga.
