About Funston Games

A small, independent game studio.

Funston Games is an independent game studio. We make games for the love of the game: games that are easy to pick up, fun to come back to, and full of little decisions that matter.

Our flagship game is Rally the Realm, a real-time battle game with chunky cartoon Vikings and hand-painted maps. Every level was playtested again and again until it felt just right. It is the first of many: more games are on the way.

What we believe

  • Fair fun. Everything in our games can be earned by playing. Buying something is a shortcut, never the only way.
  • Your information stays yours. We never sell anything about you, ads in our games are always optional, and whether they are personalized is your choice.
  • Polish matters. We would rather ship one game that feels great than five that feel rushed.
