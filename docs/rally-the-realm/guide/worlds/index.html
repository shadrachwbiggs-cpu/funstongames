The Worlds

Eight worlds, nine levels each, 72 battles in all. Every map is painted by hand.

Opening worlds and levels

  • Greenwood is open from the start.
  • Each new world opens when you earn 18 stars in the world before it. There are 27 stars in each world, so you do not need them all.
  • Short on stars? You can open the next world early with gold instead.
  • Inside a world, each level opens when you beat the one before it on any rank.

  • 1. Greenwood

    Forests, rivers, and waterfalls. Your first lessons happen here, and new buildings arrive a pair at a time: castles and forges, then rune towers and stables.

  • 2. Sunscorch Dunes

    Open desert with long sandy routes. Stables shine here, because riders cross the dunes fast.

  • 3. Frostfang Glacier

    Icy narrows and rope bridges. Holding ground matters, so castles and forges take center stage.

  • 4. Coral Isles

    Islands joined by bridges and causeways. Rune towers guard the crossings, and lone houses sit on the outposts.

  • 5. Emberpeak

    A volcano with rivers of lava. The long way around rewards fast riders and hard-hitting forges.

  • 6. Highcrag

    High ground and narrow ridges. Guard the chokepoints with castles and rune towers.

  • 7. Old Ruins

    Ancient strongholds full of old magic. Castles and rune towers rule the overgrown stones.

  • 8. Mistmarsh

    Long boardwalks and little island hamlets in the mist. Riders and houses lead the way to the final battle of the campaign.

Every world plays fair

Lava, ice, water, and mist are scenery. Every world uses the same rules; what changes is the painting, the layout, and how tough the rivals are. After Greenwood, every level has three rival clans, and every building type can appear from the first level on.
