This page is about this website. Each of our games has its own privacy policy, for example the Rally the Realm privacy policy.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. It has no forms and no accounts. We do not load fonts or scripts from other companies.

Our web host

This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure. We do not see or use that information. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We do not add you to any mailing list.
