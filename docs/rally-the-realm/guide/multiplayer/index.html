Multiplayer

Battle the people you know, wherever they live.

Hosting a battle

  1. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (in your device’s Settings app).
  2. From the main menu, tap Multiplayer, then Host a Battle.
  3. Pick any campaign level you have beaten, and the rank for any computer rivals.
  4. Invite your Game Center friends, or send an invite link in Messages.
  5. When everyone is in, choose how many computer rivals fill the empty seats, then tap Start Battle.

Battles are for 2 to 4 people, by invite only. There are no open rooms and no strangers.

How it plays

  • Everyone sees themselves as red. Seats are picked at random, and every starting building gets the same size and troops, so no one starts ahead.
  • Everyone plays with plain stats: no store upgrades, and 1 charge of each spell for the battle. Your saved spells are not used.
  • Names come from each player’s saga name. A small label under the realm bar shows who is who.
  • There is no pause in multiplayer. The corner button leaves the battle.
  • If someone leaves or loses their connection, a computer rival takes over their side so the battle can finish.
  • Tap Rematch at the end to play again with the same group.
  • Multiplayer battles do not change your saga: no stars, gold, trophies, or best times, won or lost.
