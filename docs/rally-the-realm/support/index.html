Help and Support

Stuck, confused, or found a bug? We are here to help.

Contact us

Email support@funstongames.com. Please tell us your device, which level or screen, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

Which devices can play?

Any iPhone or iPad that runs iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later: iPhone 11 and newer, iPhone SE (2nd generation) and newer, and iPad (8th generation), iPad mini (5th generation), iPad Air (3rd generation), every iPad Pro 11-inch, and iPad Pro 12.9-inch (3rd generation) and newer.

How do I play?

Greenwood levels 1 to 4 teach you step by step on Raider rank. You can replay the tips any time from Settings. The Game Guide covers everything else.

I’m stuck on a level.

Try an easier rank first: each level opens the next one when you beat it on any rank. You can also open the next level with gold, from Greenwood 5 on. See Ranks, Stars, and Gold.

Why didn’t I get gold for that win?

Gold comes from your first win on each rank, and from beating your best time by a real margin. Ordinary replays pay nothing, and the results screen tells you why.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Your progress is saved on your device and included in your device’s normal backup. Restore your new iPhone or iPad from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Apple’s Quick Start, and your sagas come along. Deleting the app deletes its saves, so please do not delete it if you want to keep your progress.

Multiplayer invites don’t arrive.

Everyone needs to be signed in to Game Center in the device’s Settings app and running the same version of the game. Update from the App Store, then try again. Check that Screen Time is not limiting multiplayer games.

I can’t hear any sound.

The game follows your device’s silent switch and volume. Music and sound effects also have their own switches in the game’s Settings. If you are playing your own music or a podcast, the game’s music stays quiet so it does not interrupt.

A purchase went wrong.

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For anything else, email us with the date and what you bought.
