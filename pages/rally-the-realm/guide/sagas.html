Sagas and Challenges

What is a saga?

A saga is one player’s adventure. You can keep up to five sagas on one device, so everyone in the family can play their own way. The first time you play, the game asks “What do they call you, Jarl?” and names your first saga.

Each saga keeps its own stars, gold, upgrades, spell charges, trophies, best times, and unlocked worlds. Settings are shared. Gold never moves from one saga to another.

Tap your saga’s name above the Campaign button to switch sagas, begin a new one, or rename one.

Deleting a saga, or restarting one from Settings, erases everything in it for good. The game asks you to type the saga’s name first, just to be sure.

Challenge rules

When you begin a new saga, you can pick any mix of these rules. They lock in for the whole saga.

RuleWhat it doesTrophy
No SpellsNo spells in battle.Iron Oath
No UpgradesNo store upgrades.Grandfather’s Axe
No Gold UnlocksNo opening levels or worlds with gold. Earn every one.The Long Road
Berserker OnlyEvery level is played on Berserker. A world opens once every level of the world before it is beaten.Mask of Fury
Fog of WarYou only see what your buildings, marching troops, and rune towers can see.Wanderer’s Hood
Blood FeudAll-out war. Buildings grow troops 3 times as fast and every send takes all but 1 troop. The same goes for your rivals.None yet

Finish the campaign with a rule still in place to earn its trophy. Take on any four and it becomes a Ragnarok Saga, with its own badge and the Gleipnir Unbound trophy. Take on more than four for a Ragnarok+ Saga.

Changed your mind? You can drop a rule later from the saga panel, but that saga can never earn that rule’s trophy again.

Fog of War

  • You see a circle around each of your buildings, a smaller one around your marching troops, and as far as your rune towers can shoot.
  • Land you have seen stays uncovered, but owners, troop counts, and marching troops only show while they are in view.
  • Buildings one road away from yours are marked with a faded ring, so there is always somewhere to send troops.
  • The realm bar shows a dark “unknown” part for what you have not found yet.
  • Fair warning: the rivals are not in the fog. They see the whole map, just like always.
