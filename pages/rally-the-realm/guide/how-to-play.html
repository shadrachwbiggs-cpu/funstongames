How to Play

The goal

You are red. Up to three rival clans, blue, green, and yellow, share the map with you, along with gray buildings that belong to nobody. Take buildings, grow your army, and knock every rival out. A rival is out when it has no buildings left and no troops still marching. Gray buildings can stay gray; you do not need them to win.

Each level starts with the whole map dimmed and the words “Tap anywhere to begin”. Take a look around, plan your first move, then tap to start.

Sending troops

  • Pick: tap one of your red buildings. To pick several, tap each one, or drag your finger across them.
  • Attack: tap a gray or rival building. Every building you picked sends half its troops, rounded down. A building with 40 sends 20; one with 15 sends 7.
  • Reinforce: double tap one of your own buildings to send help there.

Your troops march in a line along the painted roads and take the shortest way. Troops from different clans pass right through each other on the road. All the fighting happens at the buildings.

Buildings block the way. Troops can only walk through buildings of their own color. If a gray or rival building sits on the road to your target, take it first or find another road.

Capturing

Each building shows a tag like 14/20: 14 troops inside, room for 20. When your troops arrive at a building that is not yours, each one knocks out a defender. Bring more troops than it holds, and it turns red.

Watch out: a building keeps growing troops while the fight goes on, so bring a few extra. When you capture a building, it shrinks back to its smallest size, room for 10.

Growing troops

  • Every building except a rune tower grows one troop about every second until it is full. Big and small buildings grow at the same speed.
  • Gray buildings grow troops too, but they never attack.
  • Send in more troops than a building can hold, and the extra ones slowly drift away. Stock up only if you plan to send them out again soon.

Upgrading

Press and hold one of your buildings to upgrade it. A red arrow shows when a building is ready: it needs to be at least half full. Upgrading costs half of its room in troops (a 20 costs 10) and adds room for 10 more, up to 50. It takes two seconds, and the building does not grow troops or send them out while it works.

Upgraded rune towers also shoot farther and faster.

The battle screen

  • The clock counts down from the par time for your rank. Win before it hits zero to earn that rank’s star. After zero it keeps counting in red, and you can still win the level.
  • The realm bar across the top shows how much of the map each clan holds, by the size of its buildings. Red is always on the left.
  • The pause button stops everything and opens a menu with Resume, Restart, Quit, and sound settings. The map is hidden while paused, so no peeking!
  • Spells sit along the bottom on iPad, or down the side on iPhone. See Spells.

Quick tips

  • Grab nearby gray buildings early. More buildings means more troops.
  • Attack from several buildings at once to overwhelm a strong target.
  • An empty building is an easy capture. That goes for yours too, so do not leave your front line bare.
  • Greenwood 1 to 4 teach you everything step by step on Raider rank. You can replay the tips from Settings.
